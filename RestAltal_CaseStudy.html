01 · Project Overview

What is RestAltal?

RestAltal is India's most trusted AI-powered real estate platform, enabling users to buy, sell, and rent properties seamlessly across 500+ cities with verified listings, market insights, and integrated CRM tools.

Platform Type
Real Estate Marketplace + CRM
Target Market
India (Pan-India, 500+ Cities)
Core Users
Buyers · Sellers · Renters · Agents
Key Differentiator
AI-Powered Search & Market Analysis

RestAltal positions itself as the #1 Trusted Real Estate Platform in India, combining a consumer-facing marketplace with professional CRM tools for real estate agents. The platform covers the full property lifecycle — from search and discovery to listing, negotiation, and closing.

The design language reflects trust, clarity, and technology. A deep teal color palette communicates professionalism and dependability, while AI-driven features (price recommendations, listing optimization, portfolio analysis) add a modern, forward-thinking edge that distinguishes RestAltal from legacy real estate portals.

The product serves four distinct user personas: property seekers (buy), tenants (rent), property owners (sell/list), and real estate agents (CRM). Each persona gets a tailored flow, yet the unified design system creates a coherent brand experience throughout.

Core Problems Solved
  • Fragmented property discovery with unreliable listings and poor filtering
  • Lack of transparent pricing data and real-time market insights
  • Complex, multi-step property listing process without guidance
  • Disconnected lead management for agents — no unified CRM view
  • Absence of AI-assisted decision-making for buyers, sellers, and agents

03 · Page Analysis

Screen-by-Screen Breakdown

A thorough analysis of every screen in the RestAltal platform — from the homepage hero to the CRM dashboard — examining UX decisions, interaction patterns, and design rationale.

Homepage — Landing Experience

First impression, value proposition, and primary conversion entry points

Screen 01

The homepage opens with a hero section featuring a full-width property background image, immediately establishing visual context. The headline "Find Your Perfect Home Today" is direct and action-oriented, supported by a concise subtext articulating the AI-powered value proposition. A prominent location search bar sits centrally — the primary CTA — flanked by three quick-action buttons: Buy Property, Rent Property, and Sell Property, immediately mapping to the three core user journeys.

The stats row (50K+ Properties · 15K+ Customers · 500+ Cities) builds immediate social proof and platform credibility before the user scrolls. This is critical in a trust-dependent domain like real estate.

Below the fold, a 6-feature grid (AI Search, Verified Listings, Market Insights, Expert Support, Location Intelligence, Finance Tools) communicates the platform's full breadth without overwhelming. Each feature uses a teal icon, short title, and 2-line description — scannable and efficient.

Properties for Sale — Buy Page

Filterable property listings with sort, search, and sidebar navigation

Screen 02

The Buy page uses a two-column layout: left sidebar filters + main content grid. The sidebar houses: Listing Type toggle (For Sale / For Rent), Location input, Property Type dropdown, Price Range slider (₹0 to ₹2M+), Bedroom count selector, Bathroom selector, and a Pet Friendly toggle. This comprehensive filter set allows precise property discovery without clutter.

The header includes a Buy/Rent tab toggle for seamless mode switching, a text search field ("Search by location, property type, or features…"), and a sort dropdown (Newest First / Price: Low to High / Price: High to Low). This combination gives experienced users powerful control while keeping the interface approachable for first-time buyers.

The property cards display: price (prominent, top-left), property name, address, bed/bath/sqft row, feature tags (e.g., pool, garden view, modern kitchen), and a "View All Properties" link. The consistent card structure ensures rapid visual scanning across listings. Properties shown include a range from ₹45K (Urban Apartment) to ₹213.1M (Luxury Estate) — demonstrating segment breadth.

Properties for Rent — Rent Page

Rental listings with monthly pricing, identical filter architecture to Buy

Screen 03

The Rent page mirrors the Buy page layout precisely, demonstrating a shared component architecture that reduces cognitive load — users switching between buy and rent modes encounter no structural surprises. The key difference is pricing display: monthly amounts (₹/month) replace lump-sum sale prices.

Rental listings shown span: ₹12.3M/month (Luxury High-Rise · Downtown Business District · 1 Bed · 2,342 sqft), ₹2K/month (Cozy Studio · Boston · 450 sqft), and ₹4K/month (Suburban Family Home · 4 Bed · 2,400 sqft). This range covers student housing through premium rentals, ensuring broad audience relevance.

The amenity tags on rental cards (Sky Lounge, Concierge Service, Murphy Bed, Large Backyard, Two-Car Garage) provide at-a-glance lifestyle differentiation — a key decision factor for renters that goes beyond size and price.

Authentication — Login & Sign Up

Clean gated entry point for property listing and seller dashboard access

Screen 04

The authentication screens follow a minimal modal-style design with the RestAltal branding centered. The Login screen features Email + Password fields with a single Login CTA. The Sign Up screen adds a Confirm Password field. Both screens include a Sign In / Sign Up tab toggle for frictionless switching.

Notably, the sell flow gates listing at the auth stage with a "Login Required – Please log in to list your property" screen, which communicates the requirement clearly before asking for credentials. This reduces drop-off caused by unexpected interruptions mid-flow.

The auth design is intentionally stripped of distractions — no sidebar, no navigation links, no promotional content. The focus is entirely on conversion. The RestAltal logo and brand color at the top reinforces trust during a moment of potential hesitation.

List Your Property — 4-Step Wizard

AI-optimized listing flow with step-by-step property submission

Screen 05

The property listing flow is structured as a 4-step progressive wizard: Step 1 (Basic Info) → Step 2 (Property Details) → Step 3 (Description & Images) → Step 4 (Review & Publish). A numbered step indicator at the top provides orientation and progress feedback throughout the flow.

Step 4 delivers the AI Market Analysis panel — providing Price Recommendations, Unique Selling Points, Marketing Strategy, and Target Buyer profiling. This transforms a listing submission into an intelligent advisory experience, adding tremendous value beyond mere form submission.

05 · UX Patterns

Interaction Design Highlights

Key UX patterns that define the RestAltal experience and contribute to its ease-of-use and retention.

🤖
AI-Assisted Content Generation
The "Generate with AI" button in the listing flow and "Analyze My Property" in the review step reduce cognitive load for sellers. AI drafts property descriptions and provides pricing intelligence, transforming passive form-filling into active co-creation. This is a standout differentiator versus traditional portals.
🔐
Progressive Disclosure & Gating
Browse and search are fully public (no login required), maximizing top-of-funnel engagement. Authentication is only required when users take high-intent actions (listing a property), reducing unnecessary friction for the majority of users while protecting seller features.
📋
Multi-Step Wizard with Clear Progress
The 4-step listing wizard uses numbered indicators to eliminate "how much longer?" anxiety. Each step is logically grouped (basic → details → media → review), with Previous/Next navigation and a final summary review before publishing — following best-practice form UX for complex submissions.
🏷️
Amenity Tag System
Property cards surface 2–3 key feature tags (pool, garden view, panoramic views, smart home) below the standard metrics. This tag-based approach enables rapid lifestyle matching — buyers can visually scan for their must-haves without opening each listing, significantly improving discovery efficiency.
🔄
Persistent Buy/Rent Toggle
The Buy/Rent tab toggle appears on both listing pages and individual property cards, acknowledging that many users explore both options simultaneously. This micro-interaction eliminates dead-end navigation, keeping users in flow and surfacing relevant inventory immediately.
📈
Dashboard Empty State Design
Empty states (e.g., "No Inquiries Yet – When people inquire about your properties, they'll appear here") are designed with directional CTAs rather than dead-end messages. This maintains momentum for new users who haven't yet received activity, guiding them toward the next productive action.
06 · Design Strengths

What Works Well

Strategic design decisions that give RestAltal a competitive edge in the real estate marketplace.

Strength · 01
Cohesive Visual Identity
The teal-dominant palette is rare in Indian real estate (dominated by red/orange brands like MagicBricks/99acres), giving RestAltal instant visual differentiation. The consistent use of teal across headers, CTAs, icons, and accents creates a unified, trustworthy identity that users associate with calm authority.
Strength · 02
AI Integration at Every Touchpoint
AI is not bolted on — it's woven into the core experience: homepage search, listing description generation, price analysis, portfolio insights. This creates a product narrative of "intelligent assistance" that resonates with tech-forward buyers and sellers, justifying premium positioning.
Strength · 03
Dual-Mode Platform Architecture
Serving both end consumers (buy/rent) and professionals (agents via CRM) within a single product is architecturally ambitious. The design successfully separates these experiences through conditional navigation (CRM tab appears only post-login) while sharing a common design language.
Strength · 04
Verified Listings as Trust Signal
Prominently calling out "Verified Listings" as a core feature (rather than a footnote) directly addresses the #1 pain point in Indian real estate: fraudulent or inaccurate listings. This turns a compliance requirement into a brand promise.
Strength · 05
Finance Tools Integration
Including EMI calculators, loan comparison, and mortgage pre-approval directly in the platform reduces the need for external tools. This increases time-on-site, positions RestAltal as a one-stop platform, and captures users earlier in the financial decision journey.
Strength · 06
CRM + Marketplace Convergence
Integrating a full CRM (lead tracking, task management, pipeline analytics) within a consumer marketplace is a bold product strategy. It creates network effects — agents are incentivized to bring their leads onto the platform, increasing supply quality and velocity.
07 · Recommendations

Opportunities for Improvement

Constructive observations and actionable suggestions to elevate the RestAltal experience further.

UX & Interaction
R·01

Currency Consistency

The platform inconsistently uses ₹ (Indian Rupee) and $ (USD) — the AI Market Analysis screen shows "$0" while the rest of the product uses ₹. For an India-focused platform, all pricing must be standardized in INR with an explicit currency conversion toggle for international users.

R·02

Property Card Image Priority

Property cards currently emphasize price and text metadata. Leading with a high-quality property photograph (hero image) would significantly improve click-through rates — visuals are the primary driver of property interest, especially on mobile viewports.

R·03

Map View for Property Discovery

The Buy/Rent listing pages would greatly benefit from a toggleable map view alongside the grid view. Location is the #1 filter criterion in real estate — a spatial view enables neighbourhood exploration that the list view cannot replicate.

R·04

Saved Search & Wishlist Feature

There's no visible mechanism for users to save properties or searches. A persistent wishlist / saved search with notification alerts (price drops, new matching listings) would dramatically increase return visits and engagement depth.

Product & Feature
R·05

AI Price Context in Listing Review

The "AI Price Recommendation" currently shows $0 with a disclaimer that it needs more property details. Triggering AI analysis only after all 4 steps are completed (with actual property data) would produce far more accurate and useful recommendations, avoiding a trust-breaking $0 output.

R·06

Mobile-First Optimization

Given that 75%+ of Indian internet users are mobile-first, the sidebar filter layout (optimized for desktop) needs a robust mobile alternative — likely a bottom-sheet filter drawer or a sticky filter bar. The current design doesn't appear to have a dedicated mobile-responsive treatment.

R·07

Inquiry & Scheduling Flow

The CRM shows 0 inquiries for the seller dashboard properties. A more prominent "Schedule a Tour" or "Contact Agent" CTA directly on property detail pages (not just in the CRM backend) would bridge the gap between property discovery and agent contact, increasing inquiry velocity.

R·08

Onboarding & Persona Selection

New users currently land on a generic homepage. A lightweight onboarding flow ("Are you looking to Buy, Rent, or Sell?") would personalize the experience from the first visit, surfacing relevant content and features for each persona and improving early activation metrics.

08 · Conclusion

A Platform Built for Tomorrow's Real Estate Market

RestAltal is an ambitious and well-executed real estate platform that intelligently combines a consumer-facing property marketplace with a professional-grade CRM suite. Its visual design language — anchored in a trust-building teal palette and clean, modern typography — stands apart from competitors and communicates credibility from the first scroll.

The integration of AI at multiple touchpoints — search, listing creation, price analysis, portfolio insights — is the platform's most significant strategic asset. This isn't AI as a marketing buzzword; it's AI embedded into the product workflows where it genuinely reduces friction and adds intelligence. With refinements to currency consistency, mobile responsiveness, and the property discovery experience (map view, saved searches), RestAltal has the foundations of a truly world-class real estate technology platform.
